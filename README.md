# City Gridiron

A 3D low-poly football game that runs in the browser. One file, no dependencies.

- Play offense and defense, or simulate defense
- 32 city teams in real colors, four uniforms each
- Trades with players and draft picks, a yearly draft, player upgrades
- Five difficulty levels, from Rookie to Hall of Fame, and X-Factor abilities
- Players with position body types, their own running style and gear (visors, eye black, arm sleeves, towels, cleats, linemen cages)
- A broadcast crew with spoken play-by-play and captions, stadium intros, and stadiums modeled on each city's real building
- Accept or decline penalties, captains, a chosen kick returner, and end-of-season awards
- Coin toss, running game clock with a play clock and delay of game, three timeouts per half, and a sim-drive button
- Spin moves, interception returns and pick-sixes, and occasional injuries with backups stepping in
- Fumbles and loose balls, laterals, onside kicks, fake punts and field goals, flea flickers and reverses, two-back formations
- Audibles, hot routes, hurry-up offense and spikes; adjustable quarter length
- Weather (rain, snow, wind) and night games
- Instant replay with four camera angles and a postgame highlight reel
- A halftime show that names the player of the half, gives his stat line and replays his best plays, plus the top player on the other team
- Season stats for every player, league records, champions and a Hall of Fame
- Two-player games on one keyboard and practice drills that earn training points
- A kickoff power meter and quick games against the computer with any two teams
- Be a Pro: create one player and play his whole career, starting in high school, then a real college program and the NFL Draft, with game goals, XP, skill points, unlockable X-Factors, trade requests, retirement and a shot at the Hall of Fame
- Three different X-Factors for every position, so stars at the same position play differently
- Ten skin tones; each player keeps his own, and you can set it for anyone on your roster and for your Be a Pro player
- A broadcast-style pregame show: a stadium flyover, the visitors walking out, and each home team's own entrance (fire, smoke, flags, fireworks, towels, drums, the horn, cannons, a paper banner in high school), ending with the team jumping together
- Stadiums with lower and upper decks, suites, ribbon boards, video boards, a midfield logo and players on both sidelines; high school games are played at small bleacher fields

## Run it

Open `index.html` in a browser. To deploy, import this repo into Vercel with the default settings (no build step, no framework).

## Change it

The game's source is in `src/`. After editing, run `bash build.sh` to rebuild `index.html`.
